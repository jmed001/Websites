RSI means Recursive Self-Improvement, never “Rogue Severity Index”. Evidence of algorithm optimization does not by itself establish runaway RSI.
DOCUMENTED RSI-ADJACENT RESEARCH

AlphaEvolve

Original research ↗

Google DeepMind's AlphaEvolve demonstrated algorithm exploration and optimization with automatic evaluators. This is meaningful evidence of AI-assisted algorithm development, but not proof of unsupervised, accelerating model self-improvement.

INTERPRETATION RULE

Capabilities ≠ loss of control

Safety evidence should be evaluated against agent permissions, incentives, test setup, human supervision, exploitability, and real-world outcomes. This site does not convert disparate incidents into a made-up global danger percentage.
